About

Welcoming the future

We welcome people from all over the world to think about the future together. Each episode welcomes a guest and draws from their personal experience to better understand an essential question for the future.

Collective thinking

We believe in collective thinking to apprehend and embrace the complexity of the world. Our episodes are the fruit of a collective writing process among co-authors who come from different backgrounds.

An independent podcast

While we compose and produce the music of each of the episodes, their cover features the work of independent photographers from all over the world.

We defend a vision of the future lying on the possibility of the unexpected and the improbable.
